Users

Users and their groups come from the identity provider: users appear here when they sign in for the first time or when Microsoft Entra ID provisions them (Provisioned). Users that provisioning deactivated (Deactivated) get no e-mails and cannot sign in. Roles are assigned to groups. Remove users who left or changed teams to stop their e-mails; anybody who is still a member of a group at the identity provider comes back at the next sign-in or when they are provisioned again.
