Users

Users and their groups come from the identity provider: users appear here when they sign in for the first time or when Microsoft Entra ID provisions them (Provisioned). Roles are assigned to groups{{if .Settings.OIDCAppRoles}} and with app roles (shown below the role, as of the user's last sign-in){{end}}. Provisioned users are managed in Microsoft Entra ID: it deactivates them when they are disabled, deleted or unassigned there (Deactivated: no e-mails, no sign-in) and removes them when it deletes them for good. Remove other users who left or changed teams to stop their e-mails; anybody who is still a member of a group at the identity provider comes back at their next sign-in.
