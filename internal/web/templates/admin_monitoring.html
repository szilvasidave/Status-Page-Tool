Monitoring tools can keep the status page up to date: each integration has a webhook URL to which a tool sends its alerts. A firing alert opens an incident of the service it names – by the service's name or one of its names in monitoring tools – and the incident is resolved when the alert clears. Alerts that fire while a service is in maintenance wait until the maintenance has ended.
