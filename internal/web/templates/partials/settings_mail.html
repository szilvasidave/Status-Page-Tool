Allow sending from this mailbox only

Mail.Send lets the app send as any mailbox of your organisation. To allow only the sender mailbox, grant the permission in Exchange Online instead, with RBAC for Applications: assign the role Application Mail.Send to the app with a management scope that contains just this mailbox, and remove the Mail.Send consent in Entra ID (otherwise both apply). Exchange Online can take up to two hours to apply the change.
