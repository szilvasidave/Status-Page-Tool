Colours
Following the device setting, each visitor sees light or dark mode as their device is set. Choose a fixed appearance if your logo or colours suit only one of them. {{template "field-error" .Errors.theme_mode}}

Enter colours as hex codes such as #1f5bd8, or pick them. Leave a field empty for the default. Text on a coloured header or button is white or dark, whichever is easier to read.
