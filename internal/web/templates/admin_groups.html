Groups

Groups come from the identity provider: from the groups claim when members sign in, or provisioned from Microsoft Entra ID (marked Provisioned; their members come from provisioning). Give a group a role and choose the services its members are notified about. A user's role is the highest role of their groups{{if $.Settings.OIDCAppRoles}} and app roles{{end}}; everybody else who signs in is a viewer.
